Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy explains how Compute Genius handles information when you visit computegenius.com, send an inquiry, or book a consultation. It covers this website, not information handled under a separate client service agreement.

What we collect and why

  • Inquiries: The contact form asks for your name and email address. You can also provide your company name and a message. We use this information to respond and follow up on your request.
  • Bookings: The scheduling tool collects details such as your name, email address, selected appointment time, and anything you enter in the booking form. We use them to schedule and manage your consultation.
  • Technical information: Our hosting and booking providers may receive information such as your IP address, browser or device details, and request timestamps to operate, secure, and troubleshoot their services.

Who handles the information

Netlify hosts the site and stores contact form submissions. Submission notifications are routed to the Compute Genius intake inbox so we can respond.

Cal.com provides the calendar embedded on our Contact page. The embed connects to Cal.com when that page loads. Booking details are processed by Cal.com and made available to us so we can manage the appointment. Our email and calendar providers process information as needed to deliver replies and invitations.

Cookies and browser signals

We do not currently use Google Analytics or advertising pixels on this website. The site does not change its behavior in response to browser Do Not Track signals. Third-party services, including the embedded calendar, may use cookies or similar technologies and collect information about your interactions under their own privacy policies. See the linked provider policies for their practices across their services.

How long we keep information

There is no single retention period for inquiries and bookings. How long we keep a record depends on whether we are still responding, following up, or managing an appointment, and whether we need the record for legal obligations or to resolve a dispute. Copies of an inquiry may exist in both Netlify Forms and our intake inbox; booking records may also exist in Cal.com and our calendar.

Privacy requests

To ask about, access, correct, or request deletion of information you shared, use our contact form and start your message with “Privacy request.” We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Please do not submit identification documents, passwords, patient information, or other sensitive data through the form.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we’ll note them on this page and update the effective date above.
