Meet the team.

Two founders, one idea: make technology feel simple, secure and dependable for the people who rely on it.

Ahmed M.

Founder

10+ years in IT and cybersecurity. Ahmed helps organizations strengthen their security posture while streamlining operations through intelligent automation.

His expertise spans network and security, system administration and process optimization, with a track record of protecting sensitive data while improving efficiency.

Max M.

Co-Founder

Max has been fixing tech since 2011. He started out in retail tech support and training, and was one of 86 people picked worldwide to help open an office in Dubai.

Since then he's led an enterprise support team for Fortune 500 clients, run IT remotely for a New York company, and built a fast-growing company's IT department from the ground up, all the way through SOC 2.

These days it's Google Workspace, Mac fleets, automation and solid networks. Jira Service Management certified, and a big believer in tech you don't have to think about.
